## 📁 Understanding the Four Locations

Four kinds of locations matter most, and the app keeps them separated so you always know what you are looking at.

**Data path.** This is the main working directory where Kling AI keeps the bulk of its local information. Think of it as the home base for everything else. If you ever need to back up your setup or move it to a new computer, this is the folder you would copy.

**Configuration.** This is the file or folder that stores your settings and preferences. If something behaves unexpectedly, or if you want to start fresh with default settings, this is where you look. The app lets you jump straight to it rather than digging through hidden directories.

**Export cache.** While Kling AI works, it creates temporary files to speed things up. These are useful during a session but they pile up over time and take up disk space. The app shows you exactly where they live so you can clear them out whenever you like.

**Export folders.** These are the places where your finished work ends up. Instead of guessing which folder your exports were saved to, you open the app, click once, and you are there.
